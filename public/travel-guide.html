KASHMIR TRAVEL GUIDE

Plan Kashmir with confidence.

Practical ideas for choosing destinations, seasons, stays and experiences.

A great Kashmir trip is not about ticking every attraction. It is about choosing a few places and giving yourself enough time to enjoy them.

How to plan your first Kashmir holiday

Start with Srinagar

Give yourself at least a day for Dal Lake, Mughal gardens, old-city heritage and local shopping. A houseboat night can add a distinctly Kashmiri touch.

Then choose your mountain base

Gulmarg suits travellers looking for high-altitude scenery and seasonal adventure. Pahalgam is ideal for rivers, forests and relaxed valley days. Sonamarg works well as a scenic day excursion or mountain-focused stop.

Leave space for flexibility

Mountain weather and road conditions can change plans. A flexible itinerary lets you move activities without turning the holiday into a race.

When should you visit?

Spring is known for blossoms, summer for green landscapes, autumn for golden foliage and winter for snow. Your best month depends on the experience you want.
