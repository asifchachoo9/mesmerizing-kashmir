CROSS-HIMALAYA JOURNEY

Glimpses of Kashmir & Ladakh

10 Nights / 11 DaysSrinagar · Pahalgam · Gulmarg · Kargil · Leh · Nubra · Pangong
High-altitude sections are subject to road, permit and weather conditions. Build acclimatisation into the route.
Day-by-day itinerary
DAY 01
Srinagar · Pahalgam

Meet at Srinagar Airport and drive to Pahalgam. Lidder Valley and a relaxed evening.

DAY 02
Pahalgam local

Aru Valley and Betaab Valley, with Chandanwari subject to access and weather.

DAY 03
Pahalgam · Gulmarg

Drive toward Gulmarg, the Meadow of Flowers, with mountain scenery.

DAY 04
Gulmarg · Srinagar

Return for Shankaracharya Temple and Mughal Gardens: Nishat, Shalimar and Chashme Shahi.

DAY 05
Srinagar · Sonamarg · Kargil

Travel through Sonamarg and onward to Kargil, with Drass and the Kargil War memorial area.

DAY 06
Kargil · Lamayuru · Leh

Continue via Lamayuru, Moonland landscapes, Indus-Zanskar confluence, Magnetic Hill and Gurudwara Pathar Sahib.

DAY 07
Leh acclimatisation

Rest after arrival and explore Leh Palace and the market only as comfortable at altitude.

DAY 08
Leh · Nubra

Cross Khardung La when open and continue to Nubra Valley; optional Hunder village and camel experience.

DAY 09
Nubra · Leh · Pangong

Return toward Leh and continue to Pangong Lake when roads, permits and weather allow.

DAY 10
Pangong · Leh

Enjoy the lake in the morning before returning to Leh.

DAY 11
Departure Leh

Transfer to Leh Airport.
