GULMARG

A mountain playground in every season

Gulmarg combines meadow scenery with year-round mountain experiences. It is ideal for a day trip or an overnight stay from Srinagar.

Top tourist places

  • Gulmarg Gondola
  • Gulmarg meadow and St Mary's Church
  • Khilanmarg viewpoints
  • Drung waterfall
  • Seasonal snow activities

Nearby places

Tangmarg, Drung and Khilanmarg are easy additions, with Srinagar as the usual gateway.

BEST TIME

Dec–Mar

Snow and winter adventure.

GREEN SEASON

Apr–Jun

Meadows and pleasant weather.

QUIET SEASON

Sep–Nov

Cooler days and crisp views.
