DOODHPATHRI

A refreshing meadow escape

Doodhpathri is a scenic retreat known for open grasslands, flowing water and surrounding hills. It works well as a relaxed day trip or as part of a Yousmarg route.

Top tourist places

  • Doodhpathri meadow and stream views
  • Tangnar and surrounding hills
  • Forest roads and picnic spots
  • Seasonal wildflowers
  • Nearby Yousmarg

Nearby places

Yousmarg is the closest major pairing. Srinagar, Charar-i-Sharief and Aharbal can be considered for a wider route.

BEST TIME

Apr–Jun

Green meadows and pleasant conditions.

AUTUMN

Sep–Oct

Cooler air and changing colours.

WINTER

Dec–Feb

Snow, with access dependent on weather.
