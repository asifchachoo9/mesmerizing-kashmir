PAHALGAM

Slow down beside the Lidder River

Pahalgam is made for a slower pace, with river landscapes, forested slopes and beautiful surrounding valleys.

Top tourist places

  • Aru Valley
  • Betaab Valley
  • Baisaran meadow
  • Lidder River
  • Chandanwari

Nearby places

Aru, Betaab Valley and Chandanwari form the classic Pahalgam circuit. Srinagar and Doodhpathri can be combined before or after.

BEST TIME

Apr–Jun

Green valleys and comfortable weather.

AUTUMN

Sep–Oct

Cooler weather and mountain colour.

WINTER

Dec–Feb

Snow, subject to access conditions.
