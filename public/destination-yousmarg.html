DESTINATION GUIDE

Yousmarg

Quiet meadows, pine forests and peaceful mountain scenery away from the busiest routes.

YOUSMARG

A gentle escape into meadow country

Yousmarg is ideal when you want open landscapes and a slower day in the mountains. It works beautifully for walks, photography and relaxed family outings.

Top tourist places

  • Yousmarg meadow and pine forests
  • Nilnag Lake
  • Doodhganga streams
  • Charar-i-Sharief route
  • Local walks and horse-riding where available

Nearby places

Doodhpathri is a natural pairing with Yousmarg. Charar-i-Sharief and Nilnag can also be included depending on conditions.

BEST TIME

Apr–Jun

Fresh green meadows and comfortable weather.

AUTUMN

Sep–Oct

Cooler days and seasonal colour.

WINTER

Dec–Feb

Snow, with access dependent on conditions.
