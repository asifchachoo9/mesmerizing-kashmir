SRINAGAR

The lake city at the heart of Kashmir.

Dal Lake, Mughal gardens, heritage neighbourhoods and easy access to the valley.

WHAT TO SEE

Give Srinagar time

Srinagar works beautifully as both an arrival point and a destination in its own right.

Dal Lake & Shikara

Start with the water. A Shikara ride reveals floating gardens, houseboats and changing mountain light.

Mughal Gardens

Visit Nishat and Shalimar for terraced gardens, fountains and sweeping views across the lake.

Heritage & local life

Explore old Srinagar, markets and craft traditions, then leave time for kahwa and local food.
