KASHMIR TRAVEL GUIDE

Plan your Kashmir trip with confidence.

Practical ideas for seasons, destinations, stays and building a comfortable itinerary.

Best time to visit Kashmir

Spring brings blossoms, summer offers green valleys, autumn glows with golden foliage, and winter brings snow experiences.

How many days do I need?

Five to seven days works well for a classic Kashmir route; longer trips can add quieter valleys or Ladakh.

Which places should I combine?

Srinagar pairs naturally with Gulmarg and Pahalgam. Sonamarg, Doodhpathri and Gurez can be added depending on season and route.

Can my itinerary be customised?

Yes. Tell us your dates, group size and interests and we can build the route around you.
