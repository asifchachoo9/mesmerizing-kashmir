TRAVEL GUIDE

How Many Days in Kashmir?

Build a route that feels relaxed rather than rushed.

5–7 days for a classic first trip

Use Srinagar as the beginning and end of the journey, then add Gulmarg and Pahalgam. With extra time, add Sonamarg, Doodhpathri or a quieter valley.

Longer is better when you want variety

Eight to ten days gives more room for slower mornings, local experiences and weather flexibility.
