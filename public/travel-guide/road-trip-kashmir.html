TRAVEL GUIDE

Kashmir Road Trip Guide

Connect valleys without turning the journey into a race.

Build around a few bases

Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg make a practical foundation. Add quieter valleys only when road conditions and time allow.

Allow time for the road

Scenic drives are part of the experience, but travel time can vary. Avoid scheduling important activities immediately after long drives.
