Best Time to Visit Kashmir

Choose your season around the landscapes and experiences you want most.

Four distinct seasons

Spring is ideal for blossoms and gardens, summer brings lush green valleys, autumn adds golden foliage, and winter is the season for snow. The right month depends on whether scenery, walking, adventure or a quieter holiday is your priority.

Plan around the route

Weather can affect mountain roads and activities. Keep the itinerary flexible and leave room to move outdoor plans when conditions change.
