TRAVEL GUIDE

What to Pack for Kashmir

Build a practical bag around season, altitude and your activities.

Layer for changing conditions

Bring comfortable layers, a light waterproof outer layer and shoes suitable for walking. Cooler mountain areas can feel very different from Srinagar.

Add season-specific essentials

Winter requires warm insulation; spring and autumn benefit from layers; summer still calls for sun protection and comfortable walking clothes.
