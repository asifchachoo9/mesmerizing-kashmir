GUREZ VALLEY

Go beyond the usual Kashmir route

Gurez feels different from the classic circuit, with mountain passes, traditional villages, the Kishanganga River and wide open landscapes.

Top tourist places

  • Dawar village and river views
  • Habba Khatoon area
  • Tulail Valley
  • Razdan Pass viewpoints, subject to conditions
  • Traditional village walks

Nearby places

Dawar is the main base. Tulail is a memorable extension, while Srinagar and Bandipora are useful staging points.

BEST TIME

May–Oct

Best general window for road access and green landscapes.

SHOULDER

May & Oct

Peaceful periods, subject to access.

TRAVEL NOTE

Road dependent

Mountain access can change quickly.
