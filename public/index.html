KASHMIR TRAVEL GUIDE

Mesmerizing Kashmir: a journey through the valley of lakes and mountains

Kashmir is best experienced slowly. Begin with a quiet morning beside Dal Lake, follow the road toward green meadows and pine forests, then leave room for the unexpected—a local market, a warm cup of kahwa or a sunset over the mountains.

For a first visit, Srinagar makes a natural base for exploring the valley. Gulmarg brings sweeping mountain views and seasonal adventure, while Pahalgam offers river landscapes, forest walks and a gentler pace. If you want something more secluded, consider adding Doodhpathri or Gurez Valley to your route.

The ideal itinerary balances famous sights with time to simply be there. Our travel planners can arrange transport, stays, sightseeing and experiences into one comfortable plan, while keeping the schedule flexible enough for you to enjoy Kashmir at your own rhythm.
